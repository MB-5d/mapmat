# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vellic serves content strategists, UX and information architecture teams, SEO reviewers, agencies, engineers, and stakeholders who need a shared view of a website before making audit, redesign, migration, cleanup, or launch decisions.

## Product Purpose

Vellic turns a live website or manually created structure into a visual sitemap workspace. Users can scan, create, edit, review, comment on, capture, save, share, and export maps so teams can work from the actual site structure instead of scattered spreadsheets, crawl reports, screenshots, and meeting notes.

Success means users can quickly understand what exists, identify structural issues, make planning decisions, and hand off a clear artifact to clients or teammates.

## Brand Personality

Calm, expert, product-led.

The product should feel focused, practical, and trustworthy. It should show the map and the workflow clearly, keep language specific, and avoid over-explaining what users can see.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS polish, heavy AI-looking visual effects, dense visual noise, decorative dashboard tropes, and marketing layouts that distract from the product.

Avoid making Map Insights feel like the primary product. It is a useful supporting capability, but the core story is the visual sitemap workspace.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the map central.
2. Prefer clear workflows over decorative complexity.
3. Preserve code-backed design-system decisions.
4. Make collaboration and handoff states obvious.
5. Keep marketing product-led, sparse, and concrete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Maintain keyboard-friendly controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives, readable contrast, and status cues that do not rely on color alone.
